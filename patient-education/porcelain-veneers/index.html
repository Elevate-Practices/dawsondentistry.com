Porcelain Veneers

How thin porcelain shells reshape and brighten your smile.

Book online(435) 656-1111

Press play with sound for the narration

What the video covers

  1. Here's what to expect when you get porcelain veneers.
  2. Veneers are thin porcelain shells that cover the front of your teeth. They can fix stains, chips, gaps, and uneven edges.
  3. First, a digital scan or impression captures your teeth, so we can plan the shape, length, and shade of your new smile together.
  4. Next, a very thin layer of enamel is smoothed from the front of each tooth, so the veneers sit naturally.
  5. Then each veneer is custom-crafted to match the shape and shade we planned.
  6. When they're ready, each veneer is bonded to the front of your tooth and set with a gentle blue light.
  7. Porcelain looks natural and resists stains, so your smile stays bright.
  8. Care for your veneers just like your natural teeth. Brush and floss daily, avoid biting hard objects, and if you grind your teeth, wear a night guard.
  9. If you have any questions along the way, just ask. Your dental team is always happy to help.

Common questions.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. New patients of every age are welcome. Here's what to expect at your first visit, or send an appointment request online.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Often, yes, especially for emergencies and new patients in pain. Call us at (435) 656-1111 and we'll check the next available time. Emergency dental care.

What if I haven't been to the dentist in years?

You're exactly who we love to help. There's no judgment here. We help nervous and long-overdue patients get back on track, gently and at their own pace.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major plans and will verify your benefits before your visit. Call (435) 656-1111 with your plan name and we'll walk through your coverage.

What if I don't have insurance?

The Dawson Membership is a discount plan for patients without insurance, and we have payment options that fit nearly any budget. Call and we'll walk you through them.

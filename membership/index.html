Common questions.

Is the Dawson Membership dental insurance?

No. It's a discount plan offered by our office for patients who don't have insurance. There are no claims and no insurance company to deal with.

How much does it cost?

Call us at (435) 656-1111 and our front desk will give you the current price and walk you through what's included.

Can new patients join?

Yes. New and existing patients without insurance are welcome.

What if I need more than a cleaning?

Members receive a significant discount on treatment, and we have payment options that fit nearly any budget. We'll show you the cost before anything starts.

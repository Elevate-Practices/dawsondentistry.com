Tooth-Colored Fillings in St. George, UT

Repair cavities and minor tooth damage with natural-looking composite fillings designed to blend with your smile.

What Happens During a Filling

  1. 1

    Dr. Dawson examines the tooth and reviews any necessary digital X-rays to determine the extent of the cavity or damage.

  2. 2

    The area is gently numbed to help keep you comfortable throughout treatment.

  3. 3

    The decayed or damaged portion of the tooth is carefully removed.

  4. 4

    The tooth is cleaned and prepared for the composite filling material.

  5. 5

    Tooth-colored composite is placed in layers and shaped to restore the natural form of the tooth.

  6. 6

    The filling is hardened, polished, and adjusted so your bite feels comfortable.

Why Choose Tooth-Colored Fillings?

Composite fillings are matched to the shade of your natural teeth, allowing them to blend into your smile more discreetly than traditional silver-colored fillings.

Why Treat a Cavity Early?

A small cavity is usually easier and more affordable to repair. Without treatment, decay can spread deeper into the tooth and may eventually require a larger filling, crown, root canal, or extraction.

Small Cavities Do Not Heal on Their Own

Tooth decay generally continues to progress once it reaches the inner tooth structure. Treating it early helps preserve more of your natural tooth and reduces the likelihood of more extensive dental work later.

When Might You Need a Filling?

You may need a tooth-colored filling if you have a cavity, a small chip, minor wear, a damaged older filling, or an area of tooth structure that needs repair. Some cavities cause sensitivity or discomfort, while others are only identified during an exam or on an X-ray.

What If the Tooth Needs More Than a Filling?

When a tooth has a large cavity, significant fracture, or too little healthy structure remaining, a dental crown may provide better long-term protection. We will explain what we see and recommend the most conservative appropriate treatment for your tooth.

Common questions.

Do tooth-colored fillings look natural?

Yes. Composite material can be selected and shaped to blend with the shade and contours of your surrounding teeth.

Does getting a filling hurt?

The tooth is typically numbed before treatment, so most patients feel pressure or movement rather than pain. Mild sensitivity after treatment can occur and usually improves as the tooth settles.

How long does a tooth-colored filling take?

Many fillings can be completed in one appointment. The exact time depends on the size and location of the cavity and the number of teeth being treated.

How long do composite fillings last?

Their lifespan depends on the filling's size and location, your bite, oral hygiene, diet, and habits such as grinding or chewing ice. Regular exams allow us to monitor the filling over time.

Can an old silver filling be replaced with a tooth-colored one?

In some cases, yes. Replacement may be recommended if an older filling is cracked, leaking, worn, or has decay around it. A healthy filling generally should not be replaced solely without evaluating the risks and benefits.

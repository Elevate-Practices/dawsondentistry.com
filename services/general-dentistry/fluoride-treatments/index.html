Why Fluoride Matters

Fluoride is a mineral that rebuilds and strengthens tooth enamel, making it more resistant to the acids that cause cavities. While toothpaste and St. George's water supply provide some fluoride, a professional treatment delivers a concentrated dose directly where it's needed most.

  • Strengthens weak spots in enamel before they become cavities
  • Takes just a few minutes, usually right after your cleaning
  • Completely painless — no drilling, no discomfort
  • One of the most affordable ways to prevent future dental work

Common questions.

Is fluoride treatment only for kids?

No — while it's especially valuable for developing teeth, many adults benefit too, especially those with a history of cavities or dry mouth.

How often do I need it?

Most patients get a fluoride treatment at their regular six-month cleaning, though we may recommend more frequent applications for higher cavity risk.

Is it safe?

Yes. Professional fluoride treatments use a carefully measured dose that's safe and effective for both kids and adults.

How Professional Teeth Whitening Works

  1. 1

    We evaluate your teeth and gums to make sure whitening is appropriate for your smile.

  2. 2

    We discuss the shade you would like to achieve and review any existing crowns, veneers, fillings, or bonding that will not change color.

  3. 3

    We recommend the whitening option that best fits your goals, timeline, sensitivity level, and lifestyle.

  4. 4

    Professional whitening gel is used to lift stains and brighten your natural tooth enamel.

  5. 5

    We provide instructions to help you use the treatment safely and maintain your results.

  6. 6

    You leave with a noticeably brighter smile and a plan for future touch-ups when needed.

Why Choose Professional Whitening?

Professional whitening uses stronger, dentist-supervised products than most over-the-counter options. It also allows us to customize treatment around your teeth, sensitivity, existing dental work, and desired result.

What Causes Tooth Stains?

Coffee, tea, soda, red wine, tobacco, aging, and certain foods can darken teeth over time. Professional whitening can reduce many common surface stains and brighten natural tooth enamel.

Whitening Only Changes Natural Tooth Enamel

Crowns, veneers, tooth-colored fillings, and dental bonding will not whiten with bleaching gel. If you have visible dental restorations, we will help you consider the best sequence of treatment so your final smile looks balanced and natural.

Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

Professional whitening may be a good option if your teeth appear yellow, dull, or stained and you want a brighter smile without changing the shape of your teeth. Before recommending treatment, we will check for cavities, gum irritation, enamel concerns, and restorations that may affect the outcome.

Common questions.

How long do teeth-whitening results last?

Results vary based on your habits and diet. Coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and strongly colored foods can cause new stains over time. Good oral hygiene and occasional touch-ups can help maintain your brighter smile.

Will teeth whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Some patients experience temporary sensitivity during or after whitening. Let us know if you already have sensitive teeth so we can recommend an appropriate treatment schedule and products to improve comfort.

Will whitening change the color of crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening changes the shade of natural tooth enamel but does not change the color of crowns, veneers, fillings, or dental bonding.

Is professional whitening better than store-bought products?

Professional whitening allows your dentist to evaluate your oral health, use stronger professional products, and tailor treatment to your smile. Over-the-counter products may produce more limited or uneven results.

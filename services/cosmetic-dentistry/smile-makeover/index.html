Smile Makeover in St. George, UT

A smile makeover combines carefully selected cosmetic and restorative treatments to improve the color, shape, balance, and overall appearance of your smile.

Book online(435) 656-1111

How a Smile Makeover Works

  1. 1

    We begin by listening to what you like about your smile and what you would most like to change.

  2. 2

    Dr. Dawson evaluates your teeth, gums, bite, facial features, and existing dental work.

  3. 3

    We identify the treatments that may best address your cosmetic and functional concerns.

  4. 4

    You receive a personalized plan that explains treatment options, timing, priorities, and estimated costs.

  5. 5

    Treatment is completed in a carefully planned sequence to create a balanced and natural-looking result.

  6. 6

    We provide guidance for protecting and maintaining your new smile after treatment is complete.

What Can a Smile Makeover Improve?

A smile makeover may address stains, chips, cracks, worn teeth, uneven edges, gaps, misshapen teeth, missing teeth, old dental work, and other concerns that affect the appearance or balance of your smile.

Is Every Smile Makeover the Same?

No. One patient may only need whitening and bonding, while another may benefit from veneers, crowns, or replacement of missing teeth. Your plan is based entirely on your smile, oral health, goals, timeline, and budget.

A Complete Plan—Not Unnecessary Treatment

A smile makeover does not mean treating every tooth or choosing the most extensive option. Our goal is to identify the smallest combination of appropriate treatments that can produce the result you want while protecting the health and strength of your natural teeth.

Treatments That May Be Included

Professional Teeth Whitening

Brighten natural enamel and reduce stains caused by aging, food, beverages, and tobacco.

Porcelain Veneers

Improve the shape, size, color, and balance of visible front teeth with custom porcelain restorations.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Repair cavities or replace older visible fillings with restorations designed to blend naturally with your smile.

Crowns & Bridges

Restore damaged teeth or replace missing teeth while improving strength, function, and appearance.

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth with permanent, natural-looking restorations that restore your smile and confidence.

Dentures & Partials

Replace multiple or all missing teeth with comfortable, custom-designed dentures that restore your smile and improve everyday function.

Is a Smile Makeover Right for You?

A smile makeover may be appropriate if you have several cosmetic concerns or feel unsure which individual treatment would provide the best result. Before beginning cosmetic treatment, we will evaluate your oral health and address active cavities, gum disease, or other problems that could affect the longevity of your new smile.

Common questions.

What treatments are included in a smile makeover?

A smile makeover may include professional whitening, porcelain veneers, tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, dental implants, dentures, partial dentures, or other treatments. The exact combination depends on your concerns, oral health, and desired result.

How long does a smile makeover take?

Timing depends on the treatments included. Whitening or bonding may be completed relatively quickly, while veneers, crowns, implants, or more extensive treatment may require several appointments over a longer period.

How much does a smile makeover cost?

Cost varies significantly because every plan is different. After your consultation, we will explain recommended options, estimated fees, and any available payment arrangements before treatment begins.

Will my smile makeover look natural?

That is the goal. Treatment is planned around your facial features, tooth proportions, preferred shade, and individual appearance so the result looks balanced rather than artificial.

Do I need to complete every treatment at once?

Not necessarily. Depending on your oral health and priorities, treatment may be completed in phases. We can help you determine which steps are most important and how to sequence them appropriately.

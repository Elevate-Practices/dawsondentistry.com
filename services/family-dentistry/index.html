What Family Dental Care Includes

  1. 1

    We review each patient's dental history, health concerns, comfort preferences, and goals.

  2. 2

    Routine cleanings and exams help protect teeth and identify concerns before they become more complicated.

  3. 3

    Preventive options such as fluoride and sealants may be recommended based on age and cavity risk.

  4. 4

  5. 5

  6. 6

Care for Children and Teens

Early dental visits help children become comfortable with the dental office while building healthy habits. We monitor development, prevent cavities, and explain care in a reassuring, age-appropriate way.

Care for Adults and Seniors

Adult dental needs can change over time. We provide preventive, restorative, and cosmetic options to help protect natural teeth, replace missing teeth, and maintain comfortable everyday function.

One Office for the Whole Family

Having one dental home can make scheduling, records, communication, and continuing care easier. It also allows our team to understand your family's dental history and build long-term relationships with each patient.

Personalized Care at Every Stage of Life

Dental care should change as your needs change. A young child may need help preventing cavities and feeling comfortable at the dentist, while an adult may need restorative treatment or cosmetic improvements. Older patients may need additional support for gum health, worn teeth, dry mouth, or missing teeth. We tailor recommendations to each person's current health, priorities, and comfort.

Due for a Cleaning or Exam?

Regular dental cleanings and exams are the foundation of family dentistry. These visits help remove buildup, monitor oral health, and identify cavities, gum concerns, and other problems at an earlier stage.

Common questions.

What is family dentistry?

Family dentistry provides dental care for patients at different ages and stages of life, including children, teens, adults, and seniors. Services may include preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and emergency care.

Do you see children?

Yes. Dawson Dentistry welcomes children and works to make their visits comfortable, positive, and age appropriate.

Can our family schedule appointments together?

When scheduling allows, our team can help coordinate appointments for multiple family members to make dental visits more convenient.

How often should family members visit the dentist?

Many patients benefit from a cleaning and exam every six months. Patients with gum disease, frequent cavities, or other concerns may need more frequent care.

What if someone in my family is afraid of the dentist?

Let us know when scheduling. We use a calm, comfort-focused approach and can discuss nitrous oxide sedation when appropriate.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. Call our office if a family member has significant dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or another urgent concern. We will help determine the appropriate next step and arrange an emergency dental visit when needed.
